Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

Introduction :-

In this analysis, I studied the monthly sales performance of the business using SQL. I grouped the orders by month and compared the number of orders, revenue and profit.

Key Findings :-

After running the query, I got 12 monthly records. The main findings were:

- August 2025 had the highest revenue and profit.
- July 2025 had the lowest revenue.
- There were differences in sales performance from month to month.

What I Understood :-

The results show that sales were not the same every month. August was the strongest month, while July had the lowest revenue.

This comparison helped me understand how monthly sales data can show changes in business performance.

My Suggestions :-

1. Study what made August a better month for sales.
2. Find out why revenue was lower in July.
3. Compare monthly revenue and profit regularly.
4. Plan suitable offers or promotions during months with lower sales.

Conclusion :-

Monthly sales analysis is useful for understanding business performance over time. By using SQL, I was able to identify the best and weakest months and get a better idea of the sales pattern.
