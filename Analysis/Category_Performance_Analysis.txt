Category Performance Analysis

Introduction :-

In this analysis, I compared the performance of different product categories using SQL. My main aim was to find out which categories generated the highest revenue and profit and which ones needed improvement.

Category-wise Performance :-

Category        | Revenue        | Profit
Furniture       | ₹77,192,149.18 | ₹23,321,995.20
Office Supplies | ₹63,003,824.24 | ₹19,283,502.51
Electronics     | ₹50,152,961.45 | -₹5,999,376.06
Accessories     | ₹39,569,556.75 | ₹13,180,080.38

What I Found :-

After comparing the results, I noticed a few important things:

- Furniture performed best in terms of both revenue and profit.
- Office Supplies was the second-highest category in total profit.
- Electronics was the only category that showed a loss.
- Accessories had the highest profit margin at 33.31%.
- Electronics had a negative profit margin of -11.96%.

My Suggestions :-

Based on these results, I would suggest:

1. Continue focusing on Furniture because it is performing well.
2. Check the pricing and costs of Electronics products to understand the reason for the loss.
3. Look for ways to increase Accessories sales.
4. Keep checking profit margins to understand changes in performance.

Conclusion :-

From this analysis, I found that Furniture is the strongest category in terms of revenue and profit. Electronics needs more attention because it is making a loss. This analysis helped me understand how SQL can be used to compare product categories and find areas that need improvement.
